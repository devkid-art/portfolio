## What the product does

Student Network China brings international and Chinese students together in one social network, in **English, French and Chinese**. Students find classmates, join communities for their university, city or interests, message each other, organise events, ask questions and read a practical guide to student life in China.

- Real-time messaging and video calls
- Communities, events, Q&A
- Student guide and reference content (countries, provinces, cities, universities)
- An Android app shipped as a Trusted Web Activity, with its Play Store listing in three languages

A modular monolith for the API, a separate web app and a shared package for types and validation schemas. The Android app wraps the website (TWA): one codebase for web and mobile.

## Technical choices

- **PostgreSQL for everything, no Redis.** Background jobs (pg-boss), realtime fan-out across instances (Socket.IO PostgreSQL adapter) and rate limits all run on the same database. Fewer services to host and monitor.
- **Video calls with Agora** instead of a home-made WebRTC stack.
- **Reference data kept apart** from demo data, so it can be loaded safely in production.
