## What the product does

Colisa is a complete SaaS for **import-export freight forwarders**: real-time parcel tracking, customers, employees, shipments by container or air batch, WhatsApp notifications and subscriptions, with a super-admin panel.

- **Public site**: presentation, pricing, FAQ, company sign-up, parcel tracking by code or **QR scan**
- **Company workspace**: dashboard (KPIs, 30-day chart, top destinations), parcels, shipments, customers, employees, subscription, settings
- **Customer portal**: their parcels and status
- **Super admin**: companies, plans, payments

## Technical choices

- **No external database to install**: the SQLite built into Node.js is enough to run a company.
- **Prices computed automatically**: per kilo by air, per cubic metre by sea.
- **Bulk status changes**: update a whole container at once, with one WhatsApp notification per parcel, within the plan’s quota.
- **Printable labels with QR codes** and CSV export for Excel.
- **Per-employee permissions** (parcels, customers, shipments) and a number of employees limited by plan.
