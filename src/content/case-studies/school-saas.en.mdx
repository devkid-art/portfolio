## What the product does

School SaaS runs several private schools on one platform: schools, classes, students, attendance, assessments and grades, then finance (fees, invoices, balances). The project is **under construction**.

## A phased method

The project started with **a full specification, before any code**: requirements, user journeys, permissions, data model, database schema, architecture, threat model, testing strategy, cost estimate and a seven-phase roadmap.

- **Phases 1 to 3, done**: specification, technical foundation, school management
- **Phase 4, in progress**: attendance, assessments and validated grade entry; averages and report cards wait on decisions from the pilot school
- **Phase 5, in progress**: fee catalogue, invoices and balances; payments and receipts come next

Every open decision is logged, with what it blocks and who has to make the call.
