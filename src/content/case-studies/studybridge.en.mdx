## What the product does

StudyBridge is a B2B platform for agencies that guide students to Chinese universities. It replaces spreadsheets and scattered chats with a reliable catalogue and a complete workspace.

- **Public site**: presentation, CSCA exam, conferences, news
- **Catalogue**: scholarships (types A to E), universities, self-financed programmes, filtered by degree, language and scholarship type
- **Agent workspace**: overview, wallet and top-ups, orders, students, favourites, messages, identity, settings

## Technical choices

- **An agent tier engine** from 0 to 7 stars: the tier is computed automatically from top-ups and gives up to 50% off service fees.
- **An application-fee engine** that applies the business rules (base amount, per-school surcharge, the CSC scholarship exception).
- **Stripe payments** (cards and Alipay) to top up the wallet, with a mock mode in development to test without real money.
- **API hardening**: secure headers, rate limiting, input validation.
