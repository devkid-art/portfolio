## What the product does

Réserve is a **white-label online booking SaaS** for small businesses: salons, barbers, spas, photographers, garages, consultants. It was designed first for Cameroon and French-speaking Africa.

- Every business gets **a booking page in its own colours** and a management dashboard
- Customers book **with no account and no online payment**, then manage their appointment through a secure link
- Reminders and purges run as a scheduled job

One deployable modular monolith: no Redis, no queue service, no Docker in production (Docker is only for the local database).

## Technical choices

- **Tenant isolation in the database** with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security: the app connects with a role that only sees the current business’s data.
- **IANA time zones** (Luxon) and protection against **concurrent double bookings**.
- **argon2id sessions with no external auth vendor**.
- **Full documentation**: architecture, database, security, testing, environment variables, deployment and a pre-launch checklist.

## Quality

Type checking and lint, unit and integration tests against PostgreSQL, Playwright end-to-end tests on the production build.
