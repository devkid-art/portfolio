## What the product does

The online store of The Welfare Shop, a Korean-beauty specialist, delivered with its full admin. It was **in operation**; it is no longer online today.

- **Store**: collections, rails, filters, search, slide-out cart, promo codes (including a welcome code for the first order only), WhatsApp ordering
- **Activity**: live visitors (page viewed, source, device), most searched words and “searched but not found”, products viewed, added and sold, with 7, 30 or 90-day reports compared with the previous period
- **Stock**: movement history, in-store till, CSV import, back-in-stock, margins and alerts

## Technical choices

- **Analytics with no cookies and no third party**: tracking is internal and anonymous, staff and bots are excluded, data is kept 90 days.
- **Demo data loaded only locally**; in production the store starts empty, and the dashboard offers to delete test data in one click, after an automatic backup.
- **Visual identity** taken from the logo: powder pink, black and white, with Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond and Jost.
- **Documented deployment** on Render and on a VPS.
