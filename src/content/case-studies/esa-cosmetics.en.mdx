## What the product does

A complete redesign of ESA Cosmetics’ online store in Douala, in the logo’s colours. The proposal was **fully working end to end**, but the client did not adopt it: the site online today is still the old one.

- **Store**: New in, Best sellers, Sale and Back in stock rails; category and brand filters, sorting and search; product pages and similar products
- **Slide-out cart**, free-shipping progress bar, promo codes
- **Orders** saved, then sent as a summary on WhatsApp; a floating button to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, phone and e-mail
- **Admin**: revenue, orders, stock alerts, products, categories, brands, promo codes, newsletter, site settings applied live

## Technical choices

- **Local mobile payments** with NotchPay; with no key configured, only cash on delivery is offered.
- **Stock is restored automatically** when an order is cancelled.
- **Optimised images** and S3-compatible storage, automatic backups, a VPS deployment guide.
