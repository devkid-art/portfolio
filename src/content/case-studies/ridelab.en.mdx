## What the product does

RIDELAB is an international marketplace for **vehicles, heavy equipment, parts and accessories** sourced from China. Customers browse, search and filter, then send their inquiry on **WhatsApp**. There is no online checkout in this first version: the sale is closed in conversation, with the right reference.

- Catalogue by product type, category, brand and technical specs
- Multiple currencies
- Customer accounts with password reset by e-mail
- Catalogue and team administration

## Technical choices

- **Every inquiry carries an exact product ID** (for example `RID-EQP-00021`). The seller knows at once which machine is meant, with no screenshots going back and forth.
- **Typo-tolerant search** with PostgreSQL’s trigram index (`pg_trgm`).
- **Role-based admin team**: the main owner can never be removed or demoted; new members get an activation link valid for 72 hours and usable once; deactivation signs them out immediately.
- **Photos on Cloudinary** in production, with a local fallback for development.

## Quality

Data validation with Zod and Vitest tests against a disposable database wiped on every run.
